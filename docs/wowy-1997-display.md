# 1996–97 display blend

DPM, offensive DPM and defensive DPM on the historical leaderboard, profile history and career charts use a presentation-only blend during the first regular season. On November 1, 1996, the display is Daily pregame WOWY. Its weight declines linearly to zero on April 20, 1997. Total is the sum of the blended offense and defense. If no earlier or same-day WOWY estimate exists, the original DPM is shown. Underlying published DPM and the model are not changed.

The site bundles `src/lib/server/data/wowy-pregame-1997.json`: a compact per-player, per-day extract of the WOWY Daily composite, not the public Final Cut player-game history. Its source is the publication's `composite_rapm.parquet` with SHA-256 `9f1368518bde06f73e28a73cdca00c848b8a627d0d1e4cace1695aa103cfba65`. Values are rounded to four decimal places. The extract is pinned: updating the underlying WOWY publication does not silently change historical displays. To refresh it deliberately, run `python scripts/export-wowy-1997-display.py /absolute/path/composite_rapm.parquet` with DuckDB installed and update this hash. Regenerate and verify alongside a model/publication cutover.
