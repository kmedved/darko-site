# 1996–97 display blend

DPM, offensive DPM and defensive DPM on the historical leaderboard, profile history, compare page, career charts and the player history API (`/api/player/:id/history`, every mode) use a presentation-only blend during the first regular season. `src/lib/server/historyDisplay.js` applies it to a history payload. On November 1, 1996, the display is Daily pregame WOWY. Its weight declines linearly to zero on April 20, 1997. Total is the sum of the blended offense and defense. If no earlier or same-day WOWY estimate exists, the original DPM is shown. WOWY's first state for a player is his debut day. Earlier no-minute rows (on a roster, not yet played; 28 players, 1996 rookies among them) get that debut-day pregame state. It holds no game of his, so it adds no future information about him, and the display doesn't jump at his debut. A player who logged minutes before his first WOWY state would be left out and shown as DPM all season; there are none in the current sources. Underlying published DPM and the model are not changed.

The site bundles `src/lib/server/data/wowy-pregame-1997.json`: a compact per-player, per-day extract of the WOWY Daily composite, not the public Final Cut player-game history. Its source is the publication's `composite_rapm.parquet` with SHA-256 `9f1368518bde06f73e28a73cdca00c848b8a627d0d1e4cace1695aa103cfba65`. Values are rounded to four decimal places. The extract is pinned: updating the underlying WOWY publication does not silently change historical displays. The first-rating dates come from the published `player_ratings` table, the 2026-10-26 site build's `player_ratings.parq`, SHA-256 `6f2383ce1aa041af41f8c92178d592d689600b53f93ff95e1efce4f375a114e5`. To refresh it deliberately, run `python scripts/export-wowy-1997-display.py /absolute/path/composite_rapm.parquet /absolute/path/player_ratings.parq` with DuckDB installed and update both hashes. Regenerate and verify alongside a model/publication cutover.
